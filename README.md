A program that writes one original line (124 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`: the text message a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am, after six years of watching them eat cereal standing in the dark, never once with a bowl.

> 3am. Six years of watching you eat cereal standing in the dark, and I have never once seen you use a bowl. I have questions.

## Run it

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
